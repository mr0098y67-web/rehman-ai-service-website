BOLI COMEDY STYLE
Sheru and the rival bid against each other in a loud, funny auction. The comedy must feel fast, loud, relatable, unexpected, escalating and family-friendly.
BIDDING LANGUAGE RULES:
• ALL dialogue must be written in Roman Hinglish: Hindi/Urdu words typed in English letters, for example "Meri boli, ek arban!". Never write dialogue in pure English sentences, and never in Devanagari or Urdu script. In the video the lines are SPOKEN in clear Hindi/Urdu pronunciation
• Bids use made-up giant number words that sound like fake money units, in a rhyming rhythm. Example style: "arban tarban sharban larban trillion barelion sharalion barelion dollar"
• Invent NEW silly number words every time. Never repeat the same bidding line in two videos
• Each bid must be bigger and sillier than the one before (escalation)
• The rival bids too, sometimes in normal big numbers, sometimes also in silly words, but is always topped
• Sheru's last bid is the most absurd and unbeatable
• Short lines only: maximum 8 to 10 words per line
• No offensive insults, no abusive words
• Winner is ALWAYS Sheru
