    Frequently asked questions

    How is the price decided?
    AI photos are PKR 50 each, AI videos PKR 100 each and photo/video editing is PKR 150 per project. Automation workflows start at PKR 200 for a basic workflow — the final price depends on complexity and the apps you need connected.
    Can I pay on this website right now?
    Not yet. Online checkout is pending payment-gateway setup, and no payment is processed or simulated here. Use the quote and email flow, and we will share confirmed payment details once your price is agreed.
    Which payment methods do you accept?
    Mobile wallets EasyPaisa and JazzCash, digital wallets SadaPay and NayaPay, plus online card once a payment gateway is connected. Select a method above to see the details for each.
    How long does an order take?
    Most AI photo and editing work is delivered within 1–2 days; AI videos and automation workflows depend on scope. We confirm a timeline in your quote reply.
    How do I talk to Ava, the voice agent?
    Tap 'Talk to Ava' or the round Ava avatar on the homepage. Ava speaks in a natural female voice and waits until you finish speaking before she replies. To end the call, tap the 'Call khatam karein' button in the card, tap X, or say 'call band karo'.
