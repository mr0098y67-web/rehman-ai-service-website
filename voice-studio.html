New · Android app

Voice Studio for Android

Voice cloning, dubbing and dictation that run on your own phone. No account, no server needed.

Download APK (148 MB) Source code

Voice cloning

Create speech in a chosen voice, generated on the device.

Dubbing

Re-voice video and audio into other languages.

Dictation

Speak and get text, processed on the phone.

Private by design

Models run locally. They download once, the first time you pick one.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Download the APK and allow installs from this source.
2. Open the app and pick a voice model (it downloads once).
3. Clone, dub or dictate, all on your phone.
Before you install: this is a test (debug) build for Android 10 or newer. The largest voice model needs about 8 GB of RAM. Per the upstream project, builds send crash reports to the upstream project's servers. The default voice-clone model is licensed for non-commercial use only.
